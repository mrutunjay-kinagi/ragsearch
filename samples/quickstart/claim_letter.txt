Notice of Claim - Water Damage

Policyholder: Jordan Avery
Policy number: HO-55821
Date of loss: March 3

On the morning of March 3, a supply line under the kitchen sink failed while the house was empty.
Water spread across the kitchen floor and into the finished basement below. A plumber replaced the
failed supply line the same day and confirmed that it had corroded.

Damage observed:
- Laminate kitchen flooring warped along the sink cabinet and the dishwasher.
- Basement drywall wet to a height of about 30 centimetres on two walls.
- Carpet and pad in the basement family room saturated.

A water mitigation company ran dehumidifiers for four days. Their moisture readings, the plumber's
invoice, and photographs taken before cleanup are attached to this notice.

The policyholder requests coverage for the flooring, drywall and carpet replacement, and for the
cost of the mitigation service.
